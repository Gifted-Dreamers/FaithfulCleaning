About Camibella

Helping you feel less overwhelmed at home.

Clear from the start

A few simple policies so we both know what to expect.

Everything below is shared upfront so the work stays calm and predictable for both of us.

Home preparation

  • Home reasonably picked up prior to arrival
  • Floors clear of clutter; counters accessible
  • Sink dishes minimized
  • Bed sheets ready if changing beds
  • Pets secured if necessary

Payment

  • Payment due the same day service is completed
  • $10 late fee if payment is not received within 48 hours
  • Accepted: Zelle, Venmo, or cash

Cancellation & service

  • Cancellations the night before or morning of: 50% fee
  • Illness and emergencies — communicate as soon as possible
  • 1–2 weeks notice to stop recurring service
  • Without proper notice: 25% service fee may apply

Condition & pricing

  • Pricing based on home condition at walkthrough / photos
  • Estimates adjusted with client approval if scope changes
  • Quotes are estimates until walkthrough is reviewed
  • No last-minute add-ons — additional services scheduled separately

Recurring service

  • Recurring clients receive priority scheduling
  • Skipped recurring cleans may result in additional buildup charges based on condition and time needed

Services not included

  • Biohazards · Human or pet waste · Vomit · Blood
  • Mold infestations · Pest-related debris
  • Outside areas · Garages · Exterior windows

Photo policy

  • Before / after photos may be taken for business & marketing
  • Permission is always asked before sharing publicly
  • Personal & sensitive items will always be respected

Home reset clarification

  • Home reset services (laundry, dishes, light tidying, organizing) are separate from detailed cleaning services
  • Home reset services require a 2-hour minimum booking
